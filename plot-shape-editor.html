Dhaulas Plot Shape Editor

Drag corners directly over the registry scan, lock edge lengths when you want them preserved, draw new closed bodies, and read the current area live in square feet and square yards.

Instructions

  • Drag a corner handle to reshape the selected polygon.
  • Drag inside a polygon to move the whole shape without changing edge lengths.
  • Double-click an edge to insert a new corner on that edge.
  • In draw mode, click points on the scan to create a new closed body.
  • Lock an edge in the inspector to preserve its target length while dragging adjacent corners.
